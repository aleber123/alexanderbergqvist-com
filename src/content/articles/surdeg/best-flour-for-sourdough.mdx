Flour is the raw material. An 80% hydration ciabatta lives or dies
by your flour choice. Here are the US and UK flours that actually
work for sourdough — and which to avoid.

## Quick reference

| Flour | Best for | Hydration ceiling |
|---|---|---|
| King Arthur Bread Flour | All-around white bread | 70-78% |
| King Arthur Sir Lancelot | Open crumb, ciabatta | 78-85% |
| Caputo Manitoba Oro (00) | Italian breads, pizza | 78-85% |
| Caputo Tipo 00 Pizzeria | Pizza only — too weak for sourdough | — |
| Bob's Red Mill Artisan Bread | Country loaves | 72-78% |
| Bob's Red Mill Whole Wheat | Whole-grain bread | 70-78% |
| Shipton Mill No.4 (UK) | Country loaves | 72-78% |
| Doves Farm Bread Flour (UK) | Everyday | 70-75% |
| Central Milling Type 70 | Open crumb pro | 78-85% |
| Hayden Mills Heritage | Specialty flavor | 70-78% |

## The 4 things that matter in flour

### 1. Protein content (= gluten strength)

Higher protein = stronger gluten = holds more water = open crumb.

| Flour type | Protein |
|---|---|
| Bread flour (most US brands) | 12-13% |
| All-purpose | 10-11.5% |
| King Arthur Sir Lancelot | 14.2% |
| Caputo Manitoba Oro | 13% |
| Italian Tipo 00 (pizza) | 11-12% |
| Whole wheat | 13-14% |
| Whole rye | 7-9% (different gluten type) |
| Spelt | 14% (weaker gluten despite high protein) |

Tip: check the nutrition panel. If it says "13.5g protein per 100g"
that's roughly 13.5% protein content.

### 2. Milling type

- **Refined white flour:** endosperm only — neutral, strong
- **Whole wheat:** everything included — more flavor, more water
  absorption
- **Stone-ground:** ground on stone mills — preserves more gluten
  + flavor, common in artisan brands
- **Roller-milled (industrial):** most supermarket flour — works
  but less character

### 3. Freshness

Flour loses enzyme activity (alpha-amylase) over time. Organic
flour older than 6 months can produce sluggish starters.

Rules of thumb:
- **Buy 2-5 lb bags** rather than 25 lb bulk — fresh all the time
- **Store cool and dry** — especially whole rye, which goes
  rancid fastest
- **Mark the purchase date** on bags if you stockpile

### 4. Origin and chemicals

Conventional flour can have residues of glyphosate and fungicides.
Some of these **suppress wild yeast** in your starter. It's one of
the most common reasons for "my starter suddenly stopped working."

Solution: organic bread flour. Costs $1-2 more per 5 lb bag but
makes a huge difference for sourdough fermentation.

## Detailed brand reviews — US

### King Arthur Baking Company

The default recommendation for serious US home bakers. Consistently
high quality, widely available, organic options.

**Bread Flour** (12.7%): The classic. Works for 70-78% hydration.
Consistent performance, widely stocked.

**Sir Lancelot** (14.2%): High-protein Manitoba-style. For
78-85% hydration ciabatta and country loaves. Mail-order or
specialty stores.

**Organic Bread Flour** (12.7%): Worth the extra $2/bag if
your starter is at all finicky.

**Whole Wheat** (14%): Hearty, dark. Great for country loaves.

### Bob's Red Mill

Strong organic and whole-grain lineup. More widely available in
mainstream grocery than King Arthur in some regions.

**Artisan Bread Flour** (12.5%): Good all-around. Functions
similarly to King Arthur Bread Flour at a slightly lower price
point.

**Organic Whole Wheat** (14%): Stone-ground. Excellent for
50-100% whole-wheat sourdoughs.

**Organic Dark Rye** (12%): Good for rye breads and starter
maintenance.

### Central Milling (Pro/Restaurant)

If you can find it (some Whole Foods stock it, online via
Central Milling's site). Used by Tartine, San Francisco's most
famous bakery.

**Type 70** (~12.5%): Bridges all-purpose and bread flour with
some bran left in. Stunning flavor.

**Artisan Bakers Craft** (~13%): Bread flour level, used in many
top bakeries.

### Hayden Mills (Heritage grains)

Arizona-based artisan miller, specializing in heritage wheat
varieties (White Sonora, Red Fife). Distinct flavors, higher
price, lower yields. For when you've graduated past technique
and want to explore flavor.

## Detailed brand reviews — UK

### Shipton Mill

UK's leading artisan miller. Wide selection, organic, widely
available in Waitrose, Ocado, and independent grocers.

**No.4 Organic White Bread Flour** (12.5%): The go-to for serious
UK home bakers. 72-78% hydration handles well.

**Three Malts and Sunflower** (~13%): Pre-mixed with seeds — nice
shortcut for seeded loaves.

**Light Spelt** (14%): Different beast — gentler kneading
required.

### Doves Farm

Organic specialist, broadly distributed across UK supermarkets.

**Organic Strong White Bread Flour** (~12%): Standard, reliable.
Best for 70-75% hydration.

**Organic Wholemeal Bread Flour** (~13%): Solid for whole-grain
sourdough.

### Allinson's

Big UK brand, supermarket presence. Cheaper, more variable
quality.

**Strong White Bread Flour** (~12%): Functional but less
consistent than Shipton or Doves Farm.

### Marriage's

UK regional miller (Essex). Worth seeking out if you can find it
locally.

## Detailed brand reviews — Italian (worldwide)

### Caputo (Mulino Caputo)

Naples-based, used by world's top pizzerias and increasingly by
sourdough bakers worldwide.

**Manitoba Oro Tipo 00** (13%): Excellent for 78-85% hydration.
Strong gluten, gorgeous open crumb.

**Tipo 00 Pizzeria** (11-12%): Too weak for sourdough on its own.
Use blended 70/30 with Manitoba.

**Tipo 00 Cuoco** (12.5%): The pizza/bread crossover. Solid
choice for blended doughs.

### Polselli, Le 5 Stagioni

Available via specialty Italian groceries. Generally
interchangeable with Caputo at similar protein levels.

## Concrete recommendations

### Beginner
**King Arthur Bread Flour + Bob's Red Mill Whole Rye.** Classics
that work for 95% of recipes. Costs ~$0.80 per loaf in flour.
Avoid the confusion of trying everything.

### Intermediate
Add **King Arthur Sir Lancelot** or **Caputo Manitoba Oro** for
days when you want high hydration. More protein = more open
crumb = "instagrammable" bread.

### Advanced
Build a **small collection** based on what you bake:
- Bread flour (base — King Arthur or equivalent)
- High-protein (Sir Lancelot, Manitoba Oro)
- Italian Tipo 00 (Caputo)
- Whole rye (Bob's Red Mill or specialty)
- Spelt (heritage option)
- Heritage variety (Hayden Mills, Cairnspring)

## Don't skimp on flour

This is the single most important ingredient. A great bake
cannot be saved by perfect technique if the flour is bad. Spend
10-30% more on quality organic flour — you'll save countless
failed bakes.
