There are plenty of sourdough apps in the App Store, but
most fall into one of two camps: pure formula calculators
that assume you're already an expert, or recipe apps that
forget the one thing that actually kills most starters —
**missing a feed**. Here we compare 6 options on what
matters day to day.

## TL;DR

| App | Price | Feeding reminders | Recipes | Hydration math | Bake journal | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Surdeg** | Free (Premium IAP) | ✅ Per starter | ✅ 33 built-in | ✅ Per recipe | ✅ Photos + notes | **Keeping starters alive + baking** |
| Crumb | Free / IAP | ⚠️ Basic timer | ⚠️ Few | ⚠️ Limited | ✅ | Logging bakes |
| Bread Boss | Paid | ❌ | ❌ (you build formulas) | ✅ Excellent | ❌ | Formula nerds |
| Brød & Taylor | Free | ❌ | ⚠️ Brand recipes | ❌ | ❌ | Owners of their proofer |
| Generic timer apps | Free | ⚠️ Manual timers | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | A single reminder |
| Notes / spreadsheet | Free | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ (manual) | ⚠️ Manual | You don't want an app |

## 1. Surdeg

[Surdeg on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6766092580)

**Price:** Free (Premium in-app purchase unlocks unlimited
starters and extras)

**What you get:**
- ✅ Recurring **feeding reminders per starter** — run
  several starters in parallel and never miss a feed
- ✅ **33 recipes** (Swedish and international) you can bake
  straight from the app
- ✅ **Hydration calculator** per recipe, so you hit the
  right dough consistency
- ✅ **Bake journal** — log every loaf with a photo, rating
  and notes, and learn from what worked
- ✅ Works **offline**, stores everything **locally**, no
  account required
- ✅ Clean, distraction-free design built around the real
  weekly rhythm of sourdough

**Free limits:** a capped number of active starters;
Premium removes the cap and unlocks extras.

**Best for:** anyone whose starter keeps dying from a
missed feed, and bakers who want recipes, hydration math
and a bake log in one private app.

## 2. Crumb

**Price:** Free with in-app purchases

A solid bake-logging app with a clean timeline. Good if you
mainly want to record what you baked and when. Feeding
support is more of a basic timer than a per-starter
schedule, and the recipe library is thin — so beginners
still have to source recipes elsewhere.

## 3. Bread Boss

**Price:** Paid (one-time)

The choice for formula nerds. Excellent baker's-percentage
math and scaling, and it's great once you already know what
you're doing. But there are **no feeding reminders and no
recipe library** — it assumes you bring your own formula
and your own discipline.

## 4. Brød & Taylor

**Price:** Free

A companion app for owners of Brød & Taylor's proofing and
fermentation hardware. Useful if you own their gear, with
some brand recipes, but it isn't built to manage a starter
or your bakes on its own.

## 5. Generic timer apps

**Price:** Free

Plenty of "sourdough timer" apps just set manual countdowns
for bulk fermentation or feeds. Fine as a single nudge, but
you're doing all the thinking — no per-starter schedule, no
recipes, no history.

## 6. Notes app / spreadsheet

**Price:** Free

Honestly, this is where most bakers start: a note with feed
times and a spreadsheet for hydration. It works until you
have more than one starter or want reminders — then it
quietly falls apart, which is exactly the point a dedicated
app pays off.

## How to choose

- **You keep forgetting to feed your starter** → Surdeg
  (per-starter reminders are the whole point).
- **You want recipes + reminders + a log in one place** →
  Surdeg.
- **You only want to perfect formulas and already bake
  confidently** → Bread Boss.
- **You own Brød & Taylor hardware** → their app, plus
  something else for your starter.

For most home bakers — especially anyone juggling a starter
and a busy week — the app that actually keeps your starter
alive and your recipes in one place wins. That's what
Surdeg is built for.
