Most "breathing" features live inside big meditation apps —
buried under sleep stories, courses and a subscription. But
if what you actually want is to **do a breathing exercise
right now** — Wim Hof, box breathing, 4-7-8 — a dedicated
app is faster and usually free. Here we compare 6 options.

## TL;DR

| App | Price | Breathing techniques | Free core | Tracking | Custom protocols | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Andas** | Free (Premium IAP) | ✅ Wim Hof, box, 4-7-8 | ✅ Yes | ✅ Retention + streak | ✅ Premium | **Dedicated breathwork** |
| Calm | ~$70/yr | ⚠️ Small section | ❌ | ⚠️ Streak only | ❌ | Sleep + meditation library |
| Headspace | ~$70/yr | ⚠️ Some | ❌ | ⚠️ | ❌ | Guided meditation courses |
| Breathwrk | Free / subscription | ✅ Many | ⚠️ Limited | ✅ | ⚠️ | Breathwork variety |

## 1. Andas

[Andas on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6766963076)

**Price:** Free (Premium in-app purchase unlocks custom
exercises and advanced protocols)

**What you get:**
- ✅ **Wim Hof Basic, box breathing (4-4-4-4) and 4-7-8 —
  all free**
- ✅ **Animated breathing circle with haptics**, so you can
  follow the rhythm with your eyes closed
- ✅ **Retention tracking** — log your breath-hold times and
  watch them grow over weeks
- ✅ **Streaks** to keep a daily practice going
- ✅ **Build your own exercises** with sliders for inhale /
  hold / exhale (Premium)
- ✅ Advanced protocols like **Tummo and Buteyko** (Premium)
- ✅ No subscription required for the core techniques

**Best for:** anyone who wants breathwork specifically —
not a sprawling meditation library — and likes that the
essentials are free.

## 2. Calm

**Price:** ~$70/year

The most polished meditation app, with sleep stories,
music and courses. Breathing is a small "Breathe Bubble"
feature inside a much bigger (paid) experience. Great if
you want the whole calm-and-sleep library; overkill — and
pricey — if you only want breathing.

## 3. Headspace

**Price:** ~$70/year

Excellent for structured, guided meditation courses with a
friendly tone. Breathing exercises exist but aren't the
focus, and everything sits behind a subscription.

## 4. Breathwrk

**Price:** Free with subscription

A genuinely breath-focused app with a big library of
patterns for calm, sleep and energy. Strong option — but
most of the variety unlocks with a subscription, where
Andas keeps the core techniques free.

## 5. Wim Hof Method (official)

**Price:** Subscription

The definitive Wim Hof program, with the man himself and a
full course. If Wim Hof is *all* you want and you'll commit
to the program, it's the deepest. For Wim Hof plus other
techniques in one free app, Andas is lighter.

## 6. iBreathe

**Price:** Free

A clean, minimal app for box breathing and 4-7-8. Good as a
quick free timer, but no retention tracking, streaks or
custom protocols to grow a real practice.

## How to choose

- **You want breathwork specifically, for free** → Andas.
- **You want Wim Hof + box + 4-7-8 with progress tracking**
  → Andas.
- **You want a full meditation + sleep library** → Calm or
  Headspace (and pay for it).
- **You're all-in on the official Wim Hof program** → the
  WHM app.

For most people the honest answer is: you don't need a $70
subscription to breathe better. A dedicated, free breathing
app with the proven techniques and a way to track progress
is enough — which is exactly what Andas is built to be.
