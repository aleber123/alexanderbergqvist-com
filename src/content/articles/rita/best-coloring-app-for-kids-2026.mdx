## 2. Typical "free" kids coloring apps

**Price:** Free with ads + in-app purchases

The biggest category in the App Store, and the one to be
careful with. Many show full-screen third-party ads
between pictures, bury purchase buttons where small fingers
can hit them, and collect usage data. Some are fine — but
you have to vet each one. The safe move is an app that
states up front that it has **no ads and no tracking**.

## 3. Crayola Create & Play

**Price:** Subscription

A polished, brand-name option with lots of activities and
no third-party ads. Good if your child loves Crayola and
you're happy with a subscription, though it's a broader
activity suite than a focused coloring app.

## 4. Pixel Art / Color by Number

**Price:** Free with heavy ads + IAP

Number-grid "color by number" apps are satisfying for
older kids, but the popular ones are some of the most
ad-heavy apps around — frequent video ads and pop-ups.
Better suited to ages 6+ who can navigate past them.

## 5. Colorfy / Lake

**Price:** Subscription

Beautiful apps — but they're **adult** coloring apps, with
intricate mandalas and artwork on a subscription. Lovely
for a grown-up; not designed for a 4-year-old's hands or
attention span.

## 6. Paper and real crayons

**Price:** Free

Never underestimate it. For the youngest kids, paper is
great. A coloring app earns its place when you want
screen-time that's still calm and creative, no mess, and
features paper can't match — like turning a photo into a
coloring page.

## How to choose

- **You want a safe, ad-free app for a 3–8 year old** →
  Rita.
- **You love the Crayola brand and don't mind a sub** →
  Crayola Create & Play.
- **Your older child likes color-by-number grids** → a
  Pixel Art app (and expect ads).
- **You want to color yourself** → Colorfy or Lake.

For young children the first question isn't "which has the
most pictures" — it's "is this safe to hand my kid?" An app
with no ads, no tracking and a parental gate clears that
bar before anything else. That's the standard Rita is built
to.
