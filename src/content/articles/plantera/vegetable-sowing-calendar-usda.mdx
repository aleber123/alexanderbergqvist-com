Knowing when to plant is half the battle. Here's the complete
vegetable sowing calendar adjusted by USDA hardiness zone — from
when to start seeds indoors to when each crop goes outside.

## Find your zone first

Check the USDA Hardiness Zone Map at
[planthardiness.ars.usda.gov](https://planthardiness.ars.usda.gov/)
and enter your zip code. Most of the US is zones 3-10.

The zone number tells you your minimum winter temperature, which
predicts your last frost date — the most important variable for
spring planting.

Approximate last frost dates:

| Zone | Last frost | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | June 1 | Minneapolis, Anchorage |
| 4 | May 15 | Minneapolis suburbs, Bismarck |
| 5 | May 1 | Boston, Chicago, Denver |
| 6 | April 15 | NYC, Cleveland, Salt Lake City |
| 7 | April 1 | Atlanta, Memphis, Seattle |
| 8 | March 15 | Dallas, Charleston, Portland OR |
| 9 | February 15 | Houston, Tampa, LA |
| 10 | Almost never freezes | Miami, Honolulu |

## The two big planting categories

### Cool-season crops (frost-tolerant)
Plant 2-6 weeks BEFORE last frost:
- Lettuce, arugula, spinach
- Peas, fava beans
- Radishes, beets, turnips, carrots
- Kale, cabbage, broccoli, cauliflower
- Onions, garlic, leeks

These can handle light frost. Some (kale, peas, fava beans)
prefer cool weather and bolt when summer arrives.

### Warm-season crops (frost-sensitive)
Plant 1-2 weeks AFTER last frost:
- Tomatoes, peppers, eggplant
- Cucumbers, squash, zucchini, pumpkins
- Beans (snap, dry, lima)
- Corn
- Melons, watermelon

A single frost kills these crops. Wait until soil is reliably
warm (60°F / 15°C minimum).

## Indoor seed starting

Some crops need a head start. Start indoors this many weeks
before your last frost:

| Crop | Weeks before last frost |
|---|---|
| Tomatoes | 6-8 |
| Peppers (sweet) | 8-10 |
| Peppers (hot) | 10-12 |
| Eggplant | 8-10 |
| Broccoli, cauliflower, cabbage | 4-6 |
| Lettuce, kale | 2-4 |
| Cucumbers, squash | 3-4 (or direct sow) |
| Onions (from seed) | 10-12 |
| Basil, parsley | 6-8 |

## Month-by-month calendar (zones 5-7, adjust ±4 weeks)

### January
- **Indoors:** Onions and leeks from seed (zone 5-6 only)
- **Order seeds** for the season

### February
- **Indoors:** Hot peppers
- **Order any remaining seeds**

### March
- **Indoors:** Tomatoes (zones 6-7), sweet peppers, eggplant,
  broccoli, cabbage
- **Outdoors (zone 7):** Peas, lettuce, radishes, spinach
- **Outdoors (zones 5-6):** Wait

### Early April
- **Indoors:** Cucumbers, squash, basil
- **Outdoors (zones 5-7):** Peas, lettuce, spinach, radishes,
  arugula, beets, carrots, onion sets, potatoes
- **Harden off:** Tomato/pepper seedlings

### Late April
- **Outdoors (zone 5):** Peas, lettuce, radishes, hardy crops
- **Outdoors (zones 6-7):** Continue all cool-season; start
  hardening off warm-season seedlings

### May
- **Outdoors (zones 5-7):** Plant out tomatoes, peppers,
  eggplant, beans (after last frost)
- **Direct sow:** Cucumbers, zucchini, summer squash, melons,
  corn (zones 6-7)
- **Successive sow:** lettuce, radishes, beans every 2 weeks

### June
- **Direct sow (zones 4-5):** All warm-season crops
- **Continue successive sowing**
- **First harvests:** radishes, lettuce, arugula, peas

### July
- **Direct sow for fall:** Beans, lettuce, kale, radishes,
  arugula, spinach (in shade)
- **Continue harvesting**

### August
- **Direct sow:** Spinach, kale, lettuce for fall
- **Plant garlic** (in colder zones, plant late August for
  next summer's harvest)
- **Peak harvest:** tomatoes, peppers, beans, cucumbers

### September
- **Direct sow:** Final lettuce, radishes for fall harvest
- **Plant cover crops** in cleared beds
- **Plant garlic** (zones 7+)

### October
- **Plant garlic** (everywhere)
- **Final harvests**
- **Mulch beds** for winter

### November-December
- **Plant order** for next year
- **Plan crop rotation**

## Quick reference: cool vs warm crops by month

| Month | Cool crops in | Warm crops in |
|---|---|---|
| March | Z 7+ outside | Z 8+ indoor start |
| April | All zones direct sow | Z 7+ indoor start |
| May | Last cool sowings | Z 5-7 plant out |
| June | Fall sowing starts | Z 4-5 plant out |
| July | Fall sowings | Continue care |
| August | Spinach, kale fall | Peak harvest |
| September | Final fall sowings | Harvest |
| October | Garlic | Done |

## Common timing mistakes

❌ **Planting tomatoes too early** — single frost = dead
plant. Wait!

❌ **Starting tomato seeds in January** — they get leggy
indoors over 8 weeks. 6 weeks before last frost is enough.

❌ **Skipping the "harden off" step** — moving seedlings from
indoors to outdoors without gradual sun/wind exposure causes
transplant shock and death.

❌ **All-at-once planting of lettuce** — gives you 50 heads in
2 weeks then nothing. Sow new rows every 14 days.

❌ **Forgetting fall** — many gardens stop in July when there's
3 months of good growing left. Direct-sow fall crops in
August!
