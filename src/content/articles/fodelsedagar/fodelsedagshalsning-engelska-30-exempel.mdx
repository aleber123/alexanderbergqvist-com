Att skriva födelsedagshälsning på engelska känns lätt — tills du ska
göra det. Är "happy birthday!" för avslappnat till chefen? Vad
skriver man till partnerns engelska föräldrar? Här är 30 färdiga
formuleringar för 6 olika relationer.

## För nära vänner & familj (10 exempel)

Casual, varma, från hjärtat:

1. **Happy birthday, my friend!** Hope your day is as amazing as you are.

2. **Another year, another chapter!** So glad to celebrate you today. 🎂

3. **Wishing you the happiest of birthdays!** May this year bring you everything you've been hoping for.

4. **Cheers to you!** Hope your birthday is filled with love, laughter, and cake. 🥂

5. **Happy birthday!** Can't wait to see what amazing things you do this year.

6. **HBD!** Hope today is everything you wished for. ❤️

7. **Sending you the biggest birthday hug** from across the miles. Have an incredible day.

8. **Happy birthday to my favorite human!** You deserve every wonderful thing today.

9. **Many happy returns!** Hope this year is the best one yet.

10. **Wishing you 365 days of happiness.** Happy birthday! 🎉

## Till kollega (5 exempel)

Vänliga men professionella:

11. **Happy birthday!** Wishing you a fantastic day and a great year ahead.

12. **Wishing you a wonderful birthday** and an even better year of [career/projects]!

13. **Happy birthday!** Hope you get to celebrate doing something you love.

14. **Many happy returns of the day!** Enjoy your special day.

15. **Happy birthday from the team!** May the year ahead bring you joy and success.

## Till chef (3 exempel)

Kort, formellt, respektfullt:

16. **Wishing you a very happy birthday and continued success in the year ahead.**

17. **Happy birthday, [Name].** Hope you have a wonderful day with family and friends.

18. **Many happy returns!** Wishing you health, happiness, and a great year ahead.

## Till partner (5 exempel)

Personliga, romantiska:

19. **Happy birthday to my favorite person.** Life is so much better with you in it.

20. **Another year of getting to celebrate you.** I'm the luckiest. Happy birthday, love. ❤️

21. **Happy birthday, my love.** I can't wait to make this year unforgettable together.

22. **You make every day better.** Happy birthday — here's to many more years of us. 💕

23. **Wishing you the world today** because that's what you give me every day. Happy birthday. ❤️

## Till äldre släkting (3 exempel)

Respektfullt och varmt:

24. **Happy birthday, [Grandma/Grandpa]!** Thinking of you today and wishing you all the love in the world. ❤️

25. **Wishing you a wonderful birthday and a year filled with health and happiness.**

26. **Happy birthday, [Name].** You inspire all of us. Have a beautiful day.

## Till kund eller affärskontakt (4 exempel)

Formellt, oftast utan emoji:

27. **On behalf of [Company], wishing you a very happy birthday and continued success.**

28. **Happy birthday, [Name]!** Thank you for being such a valued part of our journey.

29. **Wishing you a happy birthday and a wonderful year ahead — both personally and professionally.**

30. **Many happy returns of the day, [Name].** It's a pleasure working with you, and we look forward to another great year ahead.

## Tips för att välja rätt ton

### Frågor att ställa dig själv
1. **Hur länge känner du personen?** Mindre än 1 år → håll det
   generiskt
2. **I vilket sammanhang?** Privat = mer personligt. Jobb = mer
   neutralt
3. **Skickar du publikt (Facebook, Slack channel) eller privat?**
   Publikt = kortare och säkrare
4. **Vilken kultur?** Brittisk engelska = mer formell. Amerikansk =
   mer entusiastisk. Australisk = mer casual

### Emoji-regler
- ❤️ 🎂 🎉 till nära vänner & familj
- 🎂 ensam till kollegor
- Inga emojis till chef eller affärskontakter (med undantag — om
  ert tonalitet är casual)

### "Grattis" på olika engelska-marknader
- USA: "Happy birthday!" (standard)
- UK: "Many happy returns!" (formellt, lite gammaldags)
- Australien: "Happy birthday, mate!"
