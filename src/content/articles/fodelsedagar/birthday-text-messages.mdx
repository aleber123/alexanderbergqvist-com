You want to send a great birthday text but got stuck. Here are
30 ready messages sorted by relationship. Copy, customize the
name, and send.

## To partner / spouse

**1.** Happy birthday, my love. Thank you for being you. Let's
make today one you'll always remember. ❤️

**2.** Today I'm celebrating the person who made my life so
much better than I ever thought it could be. Happy birthday,
[name].

**3.** You only get more amazing every year. Happy birthday,
[name]. Love you.

**4.** Thanks for all the laughter, the comfort, the quiet
mornings and the wild nights. You make every day better. Happy
birthday today. ❤️

**5.** Of all the days in the year, this one is one of my
favorites — because this is the day the world got you. Happy
birthday, my love.

## To mom

**6.** Happy birthday, Mom! You're still the wisest, kindest
and funniest person I know. Thank you for everything.

**7.** Mom, no one can do what you do. Happy birthday — I'm so
grateful for you.

**8.** A small reminder that you're the world's best mom. Happy
birthday today, thinking of you. ❤️

**9.** Mom — thank you for everything you've done, do, and will
do. Big happy birthday from [your name].

**10.** Happy birthday, Mom! Hope you get truly celebrated
today — you deserve it.

## To dad

**11.** Happy birthday, Dad! Thanks for all the wisdom and bad
jokes. Both have shaped me. Hope it's a great day.

**12.** Dad, you're my steady point. Happy birthday today —
you are loved.

**13.** Big happy birthday to the world's best dad! Hope we see
each other soon, but until then: enjoy the day.

**14.** Dad — thank you for always being one call away. Happy
birthday!

**15.** Today we celebrate the person who taught me almost
everything I know. Happy birthday, Dad. ❤️

## To close friends

**16.** Happy birthday, [name]! Thanks for still putting up

**17.** Of all the people I've collected as friends over the
years, you're one of the originals. Happy birthday, weirdo. ❤️

**18.** Happy birthday to one of the few people I'd answer the
phone for at any hour. Hope today's a great one.

**19.** [Name] — you're the kind of friend most people don't
even get to have once. Happy birthday today.

**20.** Today the world gets to celebrate you and I'm just
glad I noticed. Happy birthday!

## To siblings

**21.** Happy birthday, [brother/sister]! Hard to imagine my
life without you in it. Hope today's all you wanted.

**22.** [Name], you've been there since literally day one.
Happy birthday — you're stuck with me forever.

**23.** Happy birthday to my favorite [brother/sister]

## To coworkers

**24.** Happy birthday, [name]! Hope you're getting at least a
little time off today.

**25.** Birthday wishes from the office, [name]. Hope you have
a great day with people who aren't us.

**26.** Happy birthday, [name]! May the coffee be strong and
the workday be light today.

## To formal contacts (clients, bosses)

**27.** Wishing you a happy birthday, [name]. Hope you have a
wonderful day.

**28.** Happy birthday, [name]. All the best for the year ahead.

## To older relatives (grandparents, etc.)

**29.** Happy birthday, [Grandma/Grandpa]! I think of all the
stories you've told me and feel lucky every time. Hope today
is wonderful.

**30.** Sending you so much love today, [Grandma/Grandpa].
Happy birthday — call you later this evening?

## What to skip

❌ **"HBD"** — feels lazy, especially in writing
❌ **"Many happy returns"** — too old-fashioned for most contexts
❌ **Generic copy-paste with no name** — they can tell
❌ **Long-winded apologies** if it's slightly late ("So sorry I'm
   a couple of hours late...") — just send it

## When to add a follow-up

If you want to do more than text, consider these options
according to relationship depth:

**Friends or family:**
- A phone or video call (5-10 minutes is enough)
- Posting a photo of the two of you with a caption
- A handwritten card if you live nearby

**Coworkers:**
- A coffee or small gesture at work the next day
- A team card signed by multiple people

**Formal contacts:**
- An email rather than text (more professional)
- A small business gift if the relationship justifies it

## Avoid this mistake

The biggest reason birthday texts feel "off" is sending them at
the wrong moment of the day. Sending at 11:59 PM saying "Just
made it!" comes across as forgetful. Sending at 6 AM feels
overeager.

**The sweet spot:** Between 9 and 11 AM in the recipient's time
zone. Set a calendar event the day before so you don't forget.
